# #504 원화 프롬프트

## fire

Create one finished, isolated Korean ink-brush element mark on a genuinely transparent alpha background. A single centered monochrome opaque white-ink symbol, occupying about 80 percent of the square with safe margins. Bold connected shapes, broad negative spaces and lightly uneven dry-brush edges. The silhouette must remain distinct at 12, 14, 16 and 18 pixels. Keep the interior clean and solid. No letters, numerals, calligraphy characters, card, frame, background, checkerboard, glow, shadow, particles or surrounding decoration. A compact flame with a broad heavy base and three clearly separated upward tongues. The central tongue is tallest; the side tongues lean outward. Use a chunky branching silhouette with two generous notches between the tongues. No solitary teardrop shape, circular enclosure, smoke trail or detached sparks.
