Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: a new title logo concept for the Korean dark-fantasy isometric action RPG Joseon Hunters.
Primary request: invent an original custom Korean title wordmark that combines the gravitas of fictional Joseon Korea with a restrained swagger of Korean hip-hop and fusion gugak. The playful confidence belongs to the typography; retain a serious dark-fantasy game identity.
Text (verbatim): "조선헌터스". Exactly FIVE correctly formed Hangul syllable blocks, in this exact order: 조 / 선 / 헌 / 터 / 스. Hand-designed legible lettering, not plain typeset text. Preserve syllable counters, the ㄴ batchim in 헌 and 선, and the ㅌ structure in 터. No malformed glyphs.
Scene/backdrop: clean near-black charcoal presentation background, extremely subtle paper grain only; no scene illustration. A single large centered wordmark with ample uncluttered margins, polished standalone branding exploration in landscape 3:2 composition.
Constraints: the logo itself is the subject; make it scalable, optically balanced, striking at a small thumbnail size. No characters, no weapons, no microphones, no headphones, no jewelry, no graffiti spray cans, no musical instrument illustrations, no ornamental dragon, no flames, no game UI, no mockup products, no explanatory captions, no watermarks. No extra text unless explicitly specified in the direction below.
Direction: A highly compact TWO-LINE block wordmark with top row "조선" and bottom row "헌터스", equal optical width. The lettering is heavy angular Korean woodcut lettering, the outer footprint a broad near-square stamp but NOT boxed in a frame. Roughly knife-cut slab terminals, confident thick strokes, crisp readable interiors, subtle print misregistration and restrained chipped edges. Primary solid vermilion red lettering, a very thin ivory offset ink edge and tiny black ink interruptions. It should feel like traditional Korean woodblock print colliding with underground hip-hop gig typography, fierce and contemporary rather than a ceremonial heritage emblem. No circle, no dragons, no illustration, no subtitle, no outer border. The wordmark sits centered with clear margins on charcoal; true striking two-color branding, not a poster layout.
