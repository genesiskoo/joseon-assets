SUBJECT: a wide horizontal stretch of Korean countryside between two villages, painted as one band of a long handscroll: low rolling hills in light wash at the top third, a single winding dirt road that enters at the RIGHT edge at mid-height and runs across to the LEFT edge, climbing gently toward a mountain pass at the left. A few old pine trees and reeds along the road, a small stone cairn, empty fields. No people, no animals, no buildings. Light and airy: mostly pale and middle ink, generous white space in the lower half and sky. The left and right edges must fade softly into white so it can join other bands.
