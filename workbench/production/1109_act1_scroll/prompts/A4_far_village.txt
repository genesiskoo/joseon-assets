SUBJECT: a small distant Korean village in a valley, painted as one band of a long handscroll: a line of misty mountains at the top, a few thatched-roof cottages and one tile-roofed house behind a low stone wall, two wooden village guardian poles (jangseung, faces left as plain blank wood — no carved faces drawn) at the village entrance on the left, a weeping willow, a dirt road leaving the village toward the LEFT edge. No people. Quiet, slightly desolate mood. Middle and pale ink with dark accents on roofs and the willow. The left and right edges must fade softly into white so it can join other bands.
