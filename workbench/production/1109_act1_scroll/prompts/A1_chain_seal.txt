SUBJECT: an old stone door set into a rocky cave mouth, bound shut by thick iron chains wrapped around it several times — painted ONLY with loose ink-wash brushwork like a Korean literati painting: the stone is a few broad grey wash strokes, the chains are quick confident dark brush loops (calligraphic, not mechanical, not photo-real, no metallic shine). In the middle hangs a traditional Korean horizontal bar lock (jamulsoe — a long horizontal metal bar lock), NOT a western padlock. The chain end at the lower right is thinner and frayed, with a small gap between two links. Small rocks and a few grasses at the base. Centered, filling about 55% of the canvas. Pure white background (#FFFFFF), not transparent, not black.
