SUBJECT: the mountain lord tiger (sansin-ho) sitting calmly and upright on a rock, seen from the side, head turned slightly toward the viewer's left, dignified and watchful like a guardian — not roaring, not attacking. Korean folk-painting ink style: confident body outline, dry-brush stripes, a calm long tail curled around the rock. Painted in pale to middle grey ink overall (a light, quiet presence), only the eyes' area and stripe accents slightly darker. Centered, filling about 55% of the canvas. Pure white around it.
