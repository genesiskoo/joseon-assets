SUBJECT: three separate figures standing apart in one horizontal row with wide white space between them (they will be cut out one by one), each formed from ink smoke rising from the ground: (1) a graceful young woman in hanbok seen from behind, hair in a low bun; (2) a mother seen from behind holding out her open arms as if to embrace; (3) a slender companion seen in profile from behind, head tilted kindly. All three have NO faces visible (back views), their lower hems dissolve into curling smoke at the ground, and each casts on the ground a small shadow that is clearly the shape of a fox. Middle-dark ink bodies, smoke edges feathering. Pure white everywhere else.
