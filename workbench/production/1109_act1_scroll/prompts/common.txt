MEDIUM AND RULES (most important):
This is ONE LAYER of a larger ink-wash painting that will be composited later. Paint ONLY the subject described, in black sumi ink on PLAIN PURE WHITE background (#FFFFFF). The white must be clean and flat: no paper texture, no hanji fibers, no grain, no vignette, no shadow, no frame, no border, no deckle edge. Everything that is not ink must be pure white.
Ink only, grayscale: five tonal values of sumi ink (scorched black to clear wash), real brush behaviour — wet feathering bleed (beonjim), dry scratchy brush (galpil), splashed-ink mass (balmuk). Korean / East Asian ink painting in the manner of Liang Kai's splashed-ink figures and Jeong Seon's heavy ink mountains. No color at all (except where explicitly stated).
People never have drawn faces: humans are splashed-ink silhouettes read only by hat, head-cloth, shoulders and posture. No anime, no eyes.
No text, no letters, no hanja, no calligraphy, no seal stamp, no signature, no watermark. No Japanese elements. No 3D render look, no digital glow.
