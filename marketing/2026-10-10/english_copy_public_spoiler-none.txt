Joseon Hunters

ONE-LINE DESCRIPTION
A single-player isometric action RPG where a swaggering wandering swordsman hunts monsters in a dark fantasy world inspired by Korea's Joseon era.

SHORT DESCRIPTION
Step into the boots of Doho, a wandering swordsman with a sharp blade and an even sharper tongue. Explore the village of Motgol, venture across haunted fields, and descend into monster-infested dungeons. Chain sword techniques, wield elemental talismans, and chase the next piece of loot in a dark fantasy world inspired by Korea's Joseon era.

KEY FEATURES
- Click-to-move, sword-driven combat with chained attacks and distinct skills.
- Elemental talismans with fire, cold, and lightning effects.
- A village hub, dangerous fields, and dungeons with threatening bosses.
- Loot, unidentified equipment, and character builds shaped by skills and gear.
- Joseon-inspired dark fantasy with a confident, playful lead character.
